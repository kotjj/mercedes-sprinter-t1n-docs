Diagnostic Add-On Packs

In the world of vehicle diagnostics, staying ahead of the curve is essential for repair shops and maintenance businesses. LAUNCH Europe, renowned for its cutting-edge diagnostic tools, offers a range of devices that can extend the functions of your diagnostic tool.

With LAUNCH Europe’s range of diagnostic add-on packs, you can unlock a new level of diagnostic capabilities and anti-theft solutions, enabling you to tackle any vehicle maintenance or repair task with confidence. Stay ahead of the curve and maximise your diagnostic potential with LAUNCH Europe’s innovative tools.

X-PROG 3

The X-PROG 3 key and immobiliser programmer is a powerful anti-theft solution and an ideal choice for professional repair shops and vehicle maintenance businesses. X-PROG 3 is an integrated multi-function module, which offers multiple functions in one device for fast and accurate reading and writing of data. With powerful functions, supporting Mercedes-Benz infrared key reading / writing, BMW engine ISN code reading, engine reading in BENCH mode, etc. X-PROG 3 is flexible, stable, reliable, and suitable for professional users.

HD Box 2.0

The new LAUNCH X-431 HD Box 2.0 is the new VCI standard for LAUNCH commercial vehicle diagnostics. This VCI technology offers a faster and more stable data transmission, a user-friendly visual interface, supports PassThru function and also super remote diagnosis with an optional license. You can use the brand new X-431 HD Box in combination with the following diagnostic devices X-431 EURO TURBO II, EURO PRO 5, EURO PRO 5 LITE. A stable connection to LAUNCH diagnostic devices via Bluetooth, Wifi and wired modes can be established easily. It supports the full systems diagnosis, the functionality of DTC reading, DTC clearing, data stream reading, special functions and actuation test. It covers engine software such as Mercedes-Benz, Renault, Volvo, Scania, MAN and DAF and many others. It supports more than 100 kinds of commercial vehicle software in the world, such as trucks, buses, construction machinery, agricultural machinery and is therefore the perfect solution for all kind of commercial vehicle workshops.

The optional LAUNCH HaynesPro TruckSET database software helps the technician to diagnose and localise defective components, precisely specify component installation locations and also provides electrical circuit diagrams.

O2-2 ScopeBox

Turn your X-431 EURO TAB III or EURO (ONLY) into a 4-channel oscilloscope with the help of the LAUNCH O2-2 ScopeBox. The ScopeBox captures faulty / irregular signals for curve analysis on the screen of the X-431 EURO TAB III & EURO (excludes other devices). This analysis can be used to detect various defects, e.g. in sensors, actuators and circuits. Gain valuable time in vehicle diagnostics and save costs for unnecessarily replaced components.

BST-360 Battery Tester

The LAUNCH BST-360 is a Bluetooth Battery Tester suitable for both technicians and end-users. It has an advanced conductance test function that measures the exact cold start current capacity of the vehicle battery to provide meaningful results. It also provides a battery condition indicator and an overall indicator for the vehicle’s starting and charging system.

The LAUNCH BST-360 enables the user to detect problems in the charging system accurately and in time. Adding to its compact, lightweight, portable design and practical usability is the Bluetooth wireless technology that synchronises with LAUNCH X-431 diagnostic products. It provides battery condition monitoring capability helping to identify batteries that are not performing optimally within specifications.
